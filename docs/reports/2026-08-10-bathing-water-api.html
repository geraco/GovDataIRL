Nine in ten Irish bathing water samples rated 'Excellent' in late May 2014, with Waterford accounting for over a third of all tests

  • 90% of the 50 water quality samples achieved 'Excellent' status, with only 3 samples rated 'Poor' and 2 rated 'Good'
  • Waterford beaches were sampled far more intensively than other counties, representing 36% of all tests (18 of 50), followed by Galway at 30% (15 of 50) and Dublin at 18% (9 of 50)
  • E. coli levels were very low in the majority of samples, with 30% (15 of 50) recording concentrations below the detection threshold of 10 colony-forming units per 100ml
  • All samples were taken within a narrow five-day window between 22 and 26 May 2014, capturing a snapshot of early-season water quality rather than seasonal trends
A caveat worth knowing: This dataset contains only 50 samples collected over five days in late May 2014, making it too limited to assess seasonal variation, long-term trends, or the full range of Ireland's bathing waters
