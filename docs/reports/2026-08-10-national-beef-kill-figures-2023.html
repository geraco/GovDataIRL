National Beef Kill Figures 2023

Department of Agriculture, Food and the Marine cc-by · last updated 2024-03-06T13:50:58.257372

Beef slaughter jumped 19% in 2020 and remained elevated through 2023, driven by steers and grade C carcasses

  • Weekly beef kill numbers surged 19% at year-end 2020 compared to 2019, the sharpest period-over-period increase in the dataset
  • Overall slaughter rose 18% from 2019 to 2023, climbing from 4077.43 to 4797.05 animals per week on average, with a peak of 5001.35 in 2022
  • Steers accounted for 2,373,415 animals slaughtered over the period—27% more than heifers, the second-largest category at 1,734,314
  • Grade C carcasses (also 2,373,415 animals) led all classification categories by the same 27% margin over grade E, suggesting steers are predominantly classified as grade C
A caveat worth knowing: This dataset covers only 2019–2023 and provides weekly aggregates by classification and animal type; it does not capture reasons for the 2020 spike, such as COVID-19 market disruptions, herd demographics, or policy changes
