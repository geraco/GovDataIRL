86% of Irish designated bathing areas achieved Excellent water quality in 2025, down from 92% in 2024

  • 43 of 50 designated bathing areas were classified as Excellent Quality in 2025, down from 46 of 50 in 2024
  • Mayo has the highest concentration of designated bathing areas with 14 of the 50 monitored sites (28%)
  • 46 of 50 sites face short-term pollution risks that can trigger temporary swimming restrictions
  • Only one site, Lilliput on Lough Ennell, had a Sufficient Quality rating in the current year, while 6 sites achieved Good Quality and the remainder Excellent
  • All 50 sites are classified as 'Identified Beach' type and monitored between June 1st and September 15th during the official bathing season
A caveat worth knowing: This dataset covers only 50 officially designated bathing areas and does not represent all swimming locations in Ireland; water quality at unmonitored beaches and lakes is unknown
